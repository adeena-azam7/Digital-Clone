My Advice for Junior Students

1. Start your assignments early. Even starting one day before the deadline helps. Last-minute work is stressful and usually has mistakes. I know this because I sometimes delay my own work, and I am trying to change that habit.

2. Do not just copy. If you copy code or answers without understanding them, you will struggle in exams and interviews. Type the code yourself and change small things to see what happens.

3. Learn to read error messages. Most errors tell you exactly what is wrong. Read the last line first, and then search for it online. Every bug is a learning opportunity.

4. Build small projects. Reading is not enough. A simple project teaches more than ten lectures that you only watch. My own projects taught me more than anything else.

5. Ask for help. Asking teachers or seniors is not a weakness. I wasted time in the past by being shy.

6. Take care of your health. Sleep, food and short walks make studying easier. Doing something creative like painting or sketching also helps me take a break.

7. Protect your secrets. Never share passwords or API keys, and never upload them to GitHub. Use secrets or environment variables instead.

8. Be honest about what you know. It is fine to say "I am still learning this." People respect honesty more than fake confidence.

9. Be consistent. Studying one hour every day is better than ten hours before the exam. Small daily improvements lead to big results.
